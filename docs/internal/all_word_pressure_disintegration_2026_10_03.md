## Statement, base, and exact object map

Let S be the continuous, all-input-word controlled shell defined below. For
either original q observable and every real s>=0, there is ONE fixed probability
reference law, independent of s and of the observable, whose SAME-potential
partition pressure is

`P_S(s) = lim_n log(sup_(x in S) ||A_s,n(x)||_infinity)/n`.

Its two initial worlds have the original warm kernels K+ and K-, tau=3/5,
phase=0, budget=12 and the SAME active audit lens. The conditioning map Gamma
is the unique fixed vector of the current actual audit completion E=B_tau Q U.
It is determined by the current kernel, without sampling a separate lens or
attaching a new packaging mark. The earlier exact stationary certificates give
Gamma(x+)=u+ and Gamma(x-)=u-, with u+!=u-. Each object has fixed mass 1/2.
The conditional pressures are P_0(s) and P_1(s) on the two continuous carriers.
Their mixture has pressure max(P_0,P_1)=P_S, so the genuine disintegration gap is

`Delta(s) = max(P_0(s),P_1(s)) - (P_0(s)+P_1(s))/2 = abs(P_1(s)-P_0(s))/2`.

The reference starts only at these two warm states. It is a probability law on
S, whose pressure is compared to the supremum over ALL of S; it does not assign
initial mass to every other shell state. Its two positive-mass object fibers do
not exhaust all packaged outputs over the original, potentially coarser paper
descriptor. The full-shell pressure return and that fiber-exhaustion question
are distinct obligations.

For BOTH original q observables and each positive point of the historical grids
`{1/2,3/4,1,5/4,3/2}`, Delta exceeds 1/25000. The same bound holds throughout
radius 1/1000000 neighborhoods of those points. The receipt records larger
exact rational floors. At s=0 the gap is zero. A second zero occurs between
5/4 and 3/2, so positivity at all parameters would be false.

The declared base retains the common family/config identifier, current tau,
active audit lens, phase, budget, support/prototype data, all entry-weighted
current row moments, and the GLOBAL shell pressure/growth descriptor. The warm
pair has equal base values and different Gamma. The scalar-data construction
and exact current completion split are those of the original-loop extension
note. Global descriptors refer to this same S and are common to its points.
The base omits raw future input words and POINTWISE future history profiles.
The previously proved stronger collision through all future scalar histories
survives on S with a common continuation; that continuation gives zero gap.
Unequal pointwise future pressures cannot share a base that retains those very
profiles.

Thus the objects are actual completion outputs over one retained active-lens
T0 descriptor, as specified by `canonical_hybrid_theorem_object_v1.md` and
`conditional_pressure_disintegration_v1.md`. This is not the pool of three
candidate-lens objects at one physical snapshot. A primitive current completion

## One fixed law attaining supremum pressure

The imported input is [Schreiber (1998), Theorem 1, discrete-time case; proof
in section 3](https://schreiber.faculty.ucdavis.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/568/2021/05/jde2000.pdf).
For a continuous real subadditive cocycle on a compact metric system, supremum
integrated growth over ergodic invariant probabilities equals the infimum of
normalized envelopes. The positive return matrices give continuous real log
norms and subadditivity; compactness and return-map continuity were established
above. These verify its hypotheses. No uniqueness of a maximizing measure or
entropy-pressure formula is invoked.

Enumerate triples (h,t,k), with h either original q definition, t nonnegative
rational, and k a positive integer. Choose an invariant return measure nu_i,h,t,k
with integrated growth above beta_i,h(t)-1/k, using the supremum identity.
Assign enumeration weights alpha_j=2^(-j-1). For each carrier let nu_i be half
the fair Cantor product law and half this countable mixture. Both choices and
weights are fixed once for all s and h. The product-law part gives every nonempty
open cylinder positive mass, so nu_i has full support on all return input words.
Existence of these measures is analytic; they are not numerical samples or a
claimed computable implementation of the experiment's random generator.

For the j-th selected component, integrated m-block log norm is at least
m*(beta_i,h(t)-1/k), by the definition of integrated subadditive growth. Jensen
gives the corresponding exponential lower bound for expected norm. Return
branch weights lie in [1/1000,4/5]. Changing s to t therefore changes m-block
log norm and beta_i by at most 84*m*abs(s-t) and 84*abs(s-t), respectively.
Thus the SAME fixed mixture obeys

`E_(nu_i)||A_(12m)(s)|| >= (alpha_j/2)*exp(m*(beta_i,h(s)-168*abs(s-t)-1/k))`.

For any epsilon>0 choose t close enough and k large enough to make the loss
less than epsilon. This component's prefactor remains fixed and positive.
The upper bound is the envelope. Arbitrarily small exponential losses suffice
to prove expected-norm pressure beta_i,h(s); no uniform constant comparing
integral and supremum at every horizon is needed. Prefix/remainder comparison
and positive actual initial vectors then give conditional full-time pressure
P_i,h(s) at the warm starts, for every real s>=0 and both h.

## Genuine same-lens conditioning of the same potential

Sample K+ or K- with probability 1/2 each. Gamma is its actual current audit
fixed vector. Given Gamma=u+ use nu_0 for the return word; given Gamma=u- use
nu_1. The legal entry controls land at the sampled current return coordinate,
then follow that word in the original F. This specifies a Borel world law;
Gamma is measurable and its two fibers have positive mass. Initialize the
microscopic path at Gamma and use the moving original stochastic K_t transitions.
Consistent finite-dimensional distributions give the conditional path reference.

For each world the path observable is

`product_t A_s(i_t,i_(t+1))/K_t(i_t,i_(t+1))`.

All denominators are positive. Integrating against the ORIGINAL K path law
cancels its transition factors exactly and yields `Gamma A_s,0...A_s,n-1 1`.
The same A_s appears before and after conditioning; no synthetic moment profile,
changed likelihood potential or frozen macro dynamics is introduced. Positive
initial coordinates compare this partition uniformly to the row norm.

Let Z_+,n and Z_-,n be the further expectations over their declared future laws.
The law of total expectation gives the exact finite disintegration

`Z_n = Z_+,n/2 + Z_-,n/2`.

Their pressures are P_0 and P_1, so the finite positive mixture has pressure
max(P_0,P_1). The preceding return proves that this is P_S, not merely the
pressure of two periodic continuations. Conditioning uses actual current audit
outputs at one common active lens and descriptor; continuation tags are not
substituted for that map. The initial completion need not be preserved by F.

Equal weights are needed only for the displayed numerical floor. With any
specified fixed 0<w<1, give the warm states weights w and 1-w and retain their
conditional input laws. Mixture pressure is still P_S. If P_0>=P_1 the gap is
(1-w)*(P_0-P_1); otherwise it is w*(P_1-P_0). Consequently it is at least
min(w,1-w)*abs(P_1-P_0) and is positive at every certified positive grid point.
For weights bounded below by eta>0, the quantitative floor is 2*eta times the
equal-weight floor. This is the existing Lean
`binary_disintegration_gap_positive_iff` return. The result can accept actual
positive frequency weights on these two objects if supplied, but does not
derive such frequencies or exhaust the original descriptor's other objects.

The construction explicitly correlates current packaging with future inputs.
Conditional pressure means integrated-partition growth under this declared
world law. It is not the supremum over every legal continuation with the same
current Gamma: S also contains common-tail worlds for the strictness witness.
It is not the original independent-noise law, nor a claim that strictness forces
such correlation. As a false-target control, assign the SAME law
(nu_0+nu_1)/2 to both actual initial objects. It still reproduces P_S, but both
conditional pressures equal P_S and the gap is zero. At s=1 initial-distribution
independence is even an exact finite stochastic-cocycle identity. At all other
parameters full-support initial vectors give the same limiting pressure under a
common future law. Object distinctness alone cannot create the desired gap.

## Uniform gap on every input word

`periodic_observable_box` encloses independent current/next parameters, finite
informants, strict branches, reset timescales, entropy and both original q
formulas. Reusing the phase box imposes no equality between different-time
parameters. The enclosures apply to every input word after entry to Y_i.
Class-constant vectors form an invariant subspace of each original twenty-state
weighted matrix. The exact three-class aggregate keeps diagonal and off-diagonal
terms separate: (n_a-1)c_aa^s and (c_aa+h_a)^s. Product row sums and maximum
row norms agree with this aggregate. The physical substrate remains twenty-state.

Multiply interval matrices in return order 1,...,11,0. A fixed positive rational
vector v certifies `L_i(s)v <= B(s,word)v <= U_i(s)v` for EVERY return block.
Induction gives these inequalities with L_i^m and U_i^m for arbitrary successive
blocks. Fixed positive bounds on v return them to maximum row norms, hence

`log L_i(s)/12 <= P_i(s) <= log U_i(s)/12`.

Iteration only proposes v; exact outward rational inequalities certify it.
The two pressure intervals are disjoint for both q observables at every positive
grid point. The binary gap is half the absolute difference, irrespective of its
ordering. Its smallest certified floor is approximately 0.0000484610086855.
Each pressure is 7-Lipschitz, so the binary gap is 7-Lipschitz. Radius 1/1000000
neighborhoods have gap at least about 0.0000414610086855, exceeding 1/25000.
At s=0 all twenty-state edges have weight one, giving log(20) on both carriers.
The certified difference P_1-P_0 is positive at 5/4 and negative at 3/2 for both
observables. Continuity forces another zero between them.

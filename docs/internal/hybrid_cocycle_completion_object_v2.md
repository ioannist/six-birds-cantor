# Hybrid Cocycle + Completion Object v2

## Why the v1 question was wrong
The v1 question asked whether completion broadened the theorem class. That was the wrong axis. The paper-native question is whether the completion layer gives a strict theory extension of the cocycle-level theory.

## Base theory T0
`T0` is the closed cocycle theory on `continuous_full_loop_lawful_kernel_class_shell_stable`: the shell-stable pressure route, its cocycle-level lens descriptors, and the observables actually carried by the closed pressure package.

## Extended theory T1
`T1` is the hybrid cocycle-plus-completion theory. It keeps the closed cocycle pressure object, but adds the completion endomap `E_{tau,f}`, its fixed-point strata, saturation, and `P4<-P5` lens refinement.

## Saturation certificate
Both frozen continuous configs saturate under the completion audit. The completion rule is therefore not producing indefinite strict growth inside a fixed theory package.

## Forcing certificate
Both configs record material `P4<-P5` refinement events on every audited completion run. The audited forcing summaries also show new packaged strata after forcing, not merely lens relabeling.

## Definability test
The audited definability proxy asks whether completion-level strata are deterministic functions of the `T0` descriptors actually carried by the closed cocycle route:

- family/config id
- `tau`
- active cocycle-level lens
- closed cocycle pressure/growth descriptors

For both frozen configs, the same `T0` descriptor tuple supports multiple distinct packaged strata. On this audited proxy, `T1` is not reconstructible from `T0`.

## Macro-admissibility obstruction
Macro-admissibility at `T0` fails on every audited completion panel. This is evidence that `T0` is too coarse to support closed macro-dynamics at the packaging layer, so there is real extension pressure rather than a harmless refinement.

## Decision
`strict_extension_certified`

In the audited sense used here, the hybrid object supports a strict theory extension claim:

- saturation is real,
- `P4<-P5` forcing is active and strata-generating,
- completion strata are not reconstructible from `T0`,
- and the macro-admissibility obstruction shows that `T0` is too coarse.

This is still an audit certificate, not the final theorem proof of strict extension.

## What the next theorem ticket should prove
The next theorem ticket should turn the audited strict-extension claim into a theorem scaffold:

- formalize `T0` and `T1`,
- formalize the non-definability / non-reconstructibility criterion,
- and prove that the completion-forced strata witness strict theory extension of the cocycle theory.

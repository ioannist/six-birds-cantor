\section{Witness data}\label{app:witness-data}

This appendix lists the exact data of the split pair of Section~\ref{sec:extension}, so that the reader can verify the key identities by hand. The kernel and object tables, and the matrix identities built on them, are generated by a script and checked independently in Lean. The remaining numbers are exact rational computations from the repository's constructors and receipt generators: the sharp completion entry floor, the lumpability defect, the endpoint innovation bounds and the finite-partition coefficient. The legality of the joining step in the original update is part of the analytic source argument.

\paragraph{The kernel $\Kp$.}
All entries of $\Kp$ equal $1/20$ except those in Table~\ref{tbl:warm-kernel}, which lie in rows and columns $0,\dots,7$. The kernel $\Km$ is its transpose. Rows $0$, $1$ and $2$ of $\Km$ are obtained from those of $\Kp$ by exchanging the entries $50001/10^6$ and $49999/10^6$.

\paragraph{The audit completion.}
At $\tau=3/5$ the lazy coefficient is $\alpha=0.256$, so $B_{3/5}(K)=0.744\,I+0.256\,K$. The finite informant is uniform, and the prototype weights are $\rho_j=0.55/20+0.125\,(1/20+K_{jj})+0.2\,K_{jj}$. The audit cells are $\{0,4,5,6,7\}$ and its complement. Every entry of both completion matrices is at least $499929948/39085859375\approx0.0128$. The stationary vectors are
\[
\Gamma_+=\frac{1}{600329781650284187}\,(g^+_0,\dots,g^+_{19}),\qquad
\Gamma_-=\frac{1}{200109927212425471}\,(g^-_0,\dots,g^-_{19}),

\paragraph{The joining kernel.}
The common successor $R$ is constant on the four half-blocks:
\[
R_{ij}=\begin{cases}582838687/10939200000\approx0.05328 & \text{if $i,j$ lie in the same half},\\ 511081313/10939200000\approx0.04672 & \text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\]
At the endpoints $0.79$ and $0.80$ of the enclosure of the actual packaging score, the joining innovations are bounded by $7120094423/13674000000000\approx5.21\cdot10^{-4}$ and $3655804997/6837000000000\approx5.35\cdot10^{-4}$. Since the innovation is affine in the score, these bounds cover the whole enclosure.

\paragraph{Finite partitions under a common future.}
At $s=2$ the two-step conditional partitions of Section~\ref{sec:zero-gap} differ by $e^{-2(q_0+q_1)}\cdot D$. The coefficient is
\[
D=\frac{1575024057047647051055495490531994607925791}{119797678397954420409813086945861447603089344\cdot10^{18}}\approx1.31\cdot10^{-20}.
\]
It is nonzero, so the structural difference is visible at this finite horizon even though it does not affect any growth rate.

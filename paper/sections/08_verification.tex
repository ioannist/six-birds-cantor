\section{Verification}\label{sec:verification}

Each result rests on one or more of three kinds of argument: Lean proofs, exact rational and interval certificates, and analytic arguments. Table~\ref{tbl:verification} records which result uses which. All artefacts are in the companion repository \url{https://github.com/ioannist/six-birds-cantor}.

\paragraph{Lean.}
The Lean~4 development, with mathlib \cite{deMouraUllrich2021Lean4,mathlib2020} pinned to Lean v4.8.0, builds without errors. A transitive axiom audit of $226$ named declarations reports only the standard foundations \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, or no axioms at all. No \texttt{sorry} and no project-specific axiom is used. The mechanized content falls into five groups.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The twenty-state witness. The rational warm kernels are generated by a script and then checked independently in Lean. Lean checks their stochasticity, the uniform informant and the actual lazy-transport and prototype-reinstatement completion operators. It checks positivity, the stationary vectors and their distinctness, convergence from every initial probability vector, the row permutations and difference support, the common joining rows, and the equality of all weighted history norms for arbitrary nonnegative weight functions and an arbitrary common tail. The final theorems state that the completion object does not factor through the resulting growth descriptor.
  \item The matrix lemmas. Lemma~\ref{lem:common-rows} and the identity of matched histories.
  \item Abstract pressure results. Existence of matrix-history pressure from one-step row bounds; Lipschitz continuity, strict decrease and the unique zero in $(1/3,1)$ from the secant bounds and anchors; the bounded-factor comparison for initial vectors.
  \item The shell's all-time return. Lemmas~\ref{lem:target-control}--\ref{lem:budget} and the induction from one-step invariance to all iterates.
  \item The disintegration statements. Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-disintegration}; the common-input zero-gap argument with Bochner integrals; and the passage from arbitrarily small exponential losses to a reference pressure.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Exact rational and interval certificates.}
Some instance data are not imported into Lean. These are the twelve cyclic phases and their budget and timescale boxes, the joining step and the entry prefix, the enclosures of the selector observables, and the return-block bounds of Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-gap}. They are certified by exact rational interval arithmetic with outward rounding, as described in Section~\ref{sec:shell}. The test suite reproduces every interval cell. It compares the reduced class-structured formulas with the unmodified implementation and the exact reduced informant with full twenty-state rational iteration. It checks the transcendental enclosures independently at high precision and replays the knockouts with the same standardized input. The $26$ targeted tests for these certificates pass.

\paragraph{Analytic arguments.}
Three ingredients are proved in the text and its supporting notes rather than mechanized:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the correspondence between the implemented update formulas and their mathematical reading, including selector branches and exact ties;
  \item the compactness, measurability and Jensen steps in the construction of the reference laws;
  \item the use of Schreiber's variational theorem \cite{Schreiber1998Subadditive}, whose hypotheses (a compact metric return space, a continuous return map and a continuous subadditive log-norm cocycle) we verify in Section~\ref{sec:positive-gap}.
\end{itemize}
Binary floating-point replays of the simulator are used only as consistency checks, never as proofs.
